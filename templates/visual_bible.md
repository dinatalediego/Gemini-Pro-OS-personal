## Negative Bible

Never change or introduce without explicit approval:

- character identity;
- canonical wardrobe;
- historical period / product context;
- established architecture;
- aspect ratio;
- recurring color logic;
- modern objects in historical scenes;
- text inside generated images unless required.

## Image-first approval ladder

1. Rough composition — cheap/fast image generation.
2. Character consistency — Nano Banana 2.1.
3. Environment consistency — Nano Banana 2.1.
4. Approved start/end frame.
5. Only then animate in Flow.

A video generation should not be used to solve an unresolved identity or composition problem.

- recurring characters have canonical references;
